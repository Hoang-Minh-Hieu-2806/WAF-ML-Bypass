## OOF and family-group metrics

**Status: NOT EVALUATED for the combined v1.4 artifact; the family-disjoint gate cannot be computed from the available v1.4 inputs without inventing group labels.**

- v1.4 combines HttpParamsDataset rows, lab rows, and 12,288 sampled Mendeley rows. The two large external sources have no attack-family metadata in the recorded inputs; the Mendeley source also has no family/source metadata.
- The v1.4 training script fits the combined rows directly and does not persist a family assignment or OOF predictions. A random row split would not satisfy the specified `StratifiedGroupKFold(group=family)` protocol and could leak related payloads.
- v1.1's family-disjoint OOF metrics apply to v1.1's 426-row training corpus, not to v1.4. They cannot be relabeled as v1.4 OOF results. The v1.4 external holdouts and 37-case lab runs remain separate evidence types and do not substitute for OOF.
- No family label was synthesized, no threshold was tuned, and no model was retrained for this audit.
